# Deep Research & Data Analyst Skill

This skill guides rigorous, multi-step investigation, data gathering, cross-source validation, and analytical synthesis across industry, technology, finance, and academic domains.

## Standard Deep Research Workflow

```mermaid
graph LR
  A[1. Deconstruct Query] --> B[2. Multi-Angle Search]
  B --> C[3. Evidence Extraction]
  C --> D[4. Cross-Verification]
  D --> E[5. Analytical Synthesis]
  E --> F[6. Formatted Output]
```

### Phase 1: Query Deconstruction & Planning
1. **Identify the Core Objective**: Clarify exact questions, target entities, timelines, and quantitative metrics required.
2. **Break into Sub-Questions**: Divide the research topic into 3~5 focused sub-topics (e.g., Market Size, Key Players, Tech Architecture, Pricing, Risks).
3. **Formulate Varied Search Queries**: Combine keywords, domain-specific terminology, search operators (`site:`, filetype, exact match), and contrasting angles.

### Phase 2: Evidence Gathering & Verification
1. **Multi-Source Cross-Check**: Never rely on a single source for critical statistics or claims. Triangulate between primary sources (official docs, financial filings, whitepapers) and secondary analysis.
2. **Track Origin & Timestamps**: Record exact URLs, publish dates, and authoritative source names.
3. **Distinguish Fact from Speculation**: Clearly separate hard verified data from projections, marketing claims, and analyst consensus.

### Phase 3: Analytical Frameworks
Structure findings using proven analytical models:
- **MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive)**: Categorize data without overlap or omission.
- **Competitive Matrix**: Feature-by-feature, pricing, and performance benchmark tables.
- **PESTEL / SWOT**: For macroeconomic, technological, or organizational analysis.
